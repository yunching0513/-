### 描述

```
Flowmato 是一個安靜的專注計時器。它的設計只圍繞一件事：讓你更容易開始，也更容易停下來。

■ 開始前，先把牽掛放下
按下開始時，會先出現一個空白的卸載盒。把腦中還掛著的事情寫下來——要回的訊息、還沒決定的事、等一下的下一步。寫下來之後就不必再記著了。不想寫也可以直接跳過。

■ 一次只做一件事
在待辦清單裡挑一件當作這回合的目標，它會顯示在計時器上方。完成的每一顆蕃茄都會記在那件事身上，你會清楚看到某件工作到底投入了多少。

■ 結束時，留一句話給下次的自己
每段專注結束時，可以寫下「下次從哪裡接續」。它會留在那件待辦底下，下次打開就知道從哪裡開始。

■ 看得見自己把時間花在哪
每完成一段專注都會記進歷程。月曆上顏色越深代表那天做得越多，點任一天就能看到幾點到幾點、做了哪件事。想放進自己的日曆也可以——一鍵匯出 .ics，每段專注變成一個日曆事件，Google 日曆、Apple 行事曆、Outlook 都能匯入。待辦也可以指定日期，在歷程頁直接規劃某一天要做什麼。

■ 休息就好好休息
休息時間會鎖住畫面，用一個緩慢的呼吸引導陪你度過——吸氣四秒、屏住兩秒、吐氣六秒。這幾分鐘不是拿來滑手機的。想提早結束隨時可以。

■ 三層音景，隨你疊加
・環境音：雨聲、海浪、棕噪音，蓋掉環境裡的雜訊
・和聲鋪底：沒有旋律起伏的持續織體，不會勾走注意力
・節拍：左右耳的固定頻率差（需要戴耳機）
三層各自開關、可以疊在一起，休息時會自動換成比較舒緩的組合。全部即時合成，不佔空間也不需要連網。

■ 你的資料只在你的裝置裡
沒有帳號、沒有雲端、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告。待辦、統計、設定全部存在這支手機裡，App 不會把任何東西傳出去。

■ 其他
・25 / 5 / 15 分鐘可自由調整，每 4 輪一次長休息
・自動跟隨系統深淺色，專注、短休息、長休息各有專屬配色
・今天完成幾顆蕃茄、專注幾分鐘，一眼看完
・完全免費，沒有內購

Flowmato 也有 macOS 桌面版與網頁版，同一套設計。
```

Flowmato — Focus Timer

### Subtitle / Short description

Put your worries down, then focus.

### Description
```
Flowmato is a quiet focus timer. Everything in it serves one goal: make it easier to start, and easier to stop.

■ Put it down before you start
When you hit start, a blank box appears first. Write down whatever is still on your mind — a message to reply to, a decision you haven't made, the next step you're worried about forgetting. Once it's written down, you don't have to hold it anymore. You can always skip it.

■ One thing at a time
Pick one item from your to-do list as this round's goal and it sits above the timer. Every finished pomodoro is counted against that task, so you can see exactly how much went into it.

■ Leave a note for next time
When a focus block ends, write one line about where to pick up. It stays under that task, waiting for you.

■ See where your time actually went
Every finished block is recorded. The calendar shades each day by how much you did — tap any day to see the exact blocks, when they ran and what you were working on. Export it as an .ics file and every focus block becomes a calendar event you can import into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Tasks can be assigned to a date and planned right from the history view.

■ Actually rest during breaks
Breaks lock the screen and give you a slow breathing guide — in for four, hold for two, out for six. These minutes aren't for scrolling. You can end early whenever you want.

■ Three sound layers
・Ambience: rain, ocean waves, or brown noise to cover the room
・Harmonic bed: a sustained texture with no melody to pull at you
・Beats: a steady frequency offset between your ears (headphones needed)
Each layer toggles independently and they stack. Breaks switch to a gentler mix automatically. Everything is synthesised on the fly — no audio files, no network.

■ Your data stays on your device
No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads. Tasks, stats and settings live on this phone. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

■ Also
・25 / 5 / 15 minutes, all adjustable, long break every 4 rounds
・Follows your system light/dark mode
・Free, with no in-app purchases
```
