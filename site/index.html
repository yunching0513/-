About 01 / 07

研究關注What I work on

四個彼此交疊的關注：街道的安全、城市的決策、科技的介入，以及方法本身。 Four overlapping concerns: the safety of streets, the decisions of cities, the role of technology, and method itself.

01

道路安全與零死亡願景Road safety & Vision Zero

交通死傷不是意外，而是設計與制度的結果。參與 Vision Zero Taiwan 的倡議與國際合作，把系統性安全的觀點帶回台灣的街道。 Traffic deaths are not accidents but outcomes of design and institutions. Through Vision Zero Taiwan, I work on advocacy and international collaboration to bring a safe-system view back to Taiwanese streets.

SAFE SYSTEM APPROACH

02

都市治理與再生Urban governance & regeneration

從台南鐵路地下化的決策過程，到台北市都市更新處的實務工作，關注都市政體（urban regime）如何形成、誰在其中被聽見。 From the decision-making of the Tainan railway underground project to practice at Taipei's Urban Regeneration Office — how urban regimes form, and whose voices they carry.

URBAN REGIME & PRACTICE

03

人工智慧與街道設計AI & street design

碩士論文開發「市民 AI 街道設計助手」，探索生成式工具能否讓一般市民也能參與道路安全的空間討論。 My MSc thesis developed a Citizen AI Urban Street Design Assistant, asking whether generative tools can let ordinary residents take part in designing safer streets.

CITIZEN PARTICIPATION

04

混合方法研究Mixed-method research

質性訪談與空間資料分析並行：用 R 與 Python 處理資料，用 GIS 看見分布，用訪談理解為什麼。 Qualitative interviews alongside spatial analysis: R and Python for data, GIS to see distributions, interviews to understand why.

QUALITATIVE + SPATIAL
